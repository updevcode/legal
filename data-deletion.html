Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 3 September 2026

You can ask us to delete everything we hold about you at any time, without giving a reason. This page explains exactly how.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an e-mail to info@upcodeteam.com with the subject line Data deletion request.
  2. Tell us which conversation to delete — the phone number you used on WhatsApp, or your Telegram username.
  3. Say whether you also want confirmed appointments removed from the schedule. These are stored separately and are not deleted automatically.

You can also simply write "delete my data" in the chat itself. The business owner can remove your conversation directly from the assistant's management interface.

What gets deleted

  • The full conversation history, including every message you sent and every reply.
  • Transcripts of voice messages. The original audio was never stored in the first place.
  • Your name, phone number and messenger identifier as held by the assistant.
  • Draft and pending bookings.
  • Internal records associated with the conversation.
  • Confirmed calendar appointments — only if you ask for them as well.

What may remain, and why

A small amount of data can survive a deletion request, and only where the law requires it:

  • Accounting records. If a paid transaction took place, invoices and related records are kept for the period required by Moldovan accounting and tax law.
  • Server logs. Technical logs containing IP addresses and request timestamps rotate out automatically within 90 days and are not linked back to conversation content.
  • Backups. Deleted data may persist in encrypted backups until those backups expire in the normal rotation, within 30 days. It is not restored into the live system.

How long it takes

We confirm receipt within 3 business days and complete deletion within 30 days of the request. You receive written confirmation once it is done.

Deletion is irreversible. Once a conversation is removed, neither you nor we can recover it.

Verification

To protect you from someone else deleting your data, we may ask you to confirm the request from the same phone number or messenger account the conversation belongs to. We ask for no more than is necessary for that check, and we do not keep the verification correspondence beyond it.
