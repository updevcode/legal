Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

This policy explains what data the chat assistant collects and how it is handled. The assistant operates on WhatsApp and Telegram on behalf of UPCODE GROUP, SRL, Str. Alba-Iulia 89, ap. 17, MD-2071, Chisinau, Republic of Moldova ("we", "us").

For any question about your data, write to info@upcodeteam.com.

What we receive

The assistant receives data only from you, through the conversation you started. We do not buy data, we do not collect it from other websites, and we do not track you outside this conversation.

Your messenger identifier. Your phone number as WhatsApp reports it, or your Telegram chat identifier — whatever the messenger uses to address you. Without it we cannot reply.

Your name, as set in your messenger profile and as you give it in the conversation.

Your phone number, if you state it in the conversation, so we can find your customer record and your appointment.

The content of your messages. The conversation is stored in full so the assistant remembers what was said and does not ask you the same thing twice.

Voice messages. We transcribe them to text and store the transcript only. The audio file itself is never saved: it is processed in memory and discarded as soon as it has been transcribed.

The language of the conversation, so we can reply in it.

Appointment details, if you book: the service, the provider, the time, and the price you were shown.

We do not receive your contacts, photos, location, or anything else beyond the conversation you have with us.

Why we use it

The only purpose is to hold the conversation with you and to handle bookings. We do not show you advertising, we do not build advertising audiences from your data, and we do not sell or hand your data to third parties for purposes of their own.

Customer and appointment records are kept in our own database, described below. No separate third-party customer-record system is involved.

Where the data is kept, and for how long

Conversations and customer records are stored in a database on our own server, hosted on infrastructure located in the European Union.

Retention: 24 months from the last message in the conversation. After that the conversation is deleted.

The Republic of Moldova, where we are established, is recognised by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of protection for personal data, so data may move between our establishment and our EU-hosted infrastructure without additional transfer safeguards.

Deleting your data

Write to info@upcodeteam.com and we will delete your conversation. Deletion is irreversible: it removes the conversation together with its full history, draft bookings, and the internal records about it. Confirmed appointments in the schedule are deleted separately — tell us if you want those removed as well.

The business owner can also delete your conversation directly from the assistant's management interface at your request.

Step-by-step instructions are on our Data Deletion page.

Your rights

You may ask what data of yours we hold, ask us to correct it or delete it, and withdraw your consent to processing. To stop the assistant replying to you, it is enough to stop writing to it; to remove what has already been collected, write to us.

We respond to requests within 30 days. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to confirm that the request comes from you.

If you believe we are handling your data unlawfully, you may complain to the National Centre for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Moldova, Str. Serghei Lazo 48, MD-2004, Chisinau — datepersonale.md. If you are resident in the European Economic Area, you may also complain to the supervisory authority of your own country.

Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data; if such data has reached us, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Access to the database is limited to service accounts. Connections to the messengers and to the providers listed above are protected with TLS. Incoming WhatsApp notifications are authenticated by cryptographic signature.

Nobody can guarantee complete protection, and we do not claim to. What we do promise is not to keep more than necessary: audio recordings are never stored at all, and conversations are deleted on request.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top of the page. We announce material changes in the conversation itself.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the law of the Republic of Moldova, in particular Law No. 133 of 8 July 2011 on the protection of personal data.
